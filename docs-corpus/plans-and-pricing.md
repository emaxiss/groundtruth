# Plans and Pricing

TaskLoop has three plans: Free, Pro, and Team. Prices are in USD and exclude sales tax or VAT.

## Free: $0

- 3 boards per workspace
- 5 users per workspace (pending invitations count toward this)
- 2 GB total storage, 25 MB per attachment
- 30-day activity history
- Community and in-app chat support
- No SSO, no SLA, no priority routing

## Pro: $12 per user per month

Billed monthly at $12/user/month, or annually at $120/user/year (equivalent to $10/user/month, a 17% discount).

- Unlimited boards
- 50 GB storage, 100 MB per attachment
- 1-year activity history
- Advanced filters, 50 saved filters per user
- All integrations (Slack, GitHub, Google Drive)
- API access at 100 requests per minute

## Team: $29 per user per month

Billed monthly at $29/user/month, or annually at $290/user/year (equivalent to $24.17/user/month).

- Everything in Pro
- SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning
- 500 GB storage
- 3-year activity history
- 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits
- Priority support routing
- Audit log export

## Plan mechanics

Minimum seats: Free has no minimum; Pro requires 1 seat; Team requires 3 seats. Seat counts are enforced at checkout, so a Team workspace cannot drop below 3 paid seats without downgrading.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, with prorated credit applied to the next invoice.

There is no free trial of Team. Pro offers a 14-day free trial, once per workspace, no card required.

Nonprofit and education discounts of 30% are available by contacting support with documentation. Discounts are not applied automatically and are not stackable with annual pricing.
