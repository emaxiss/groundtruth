# Billing and Invoices

## Billing cycle

Subscriptions renew automatically on the anniversary of the original charge. Monthly plans renew on the same day each month; if that day does not exist (for example the 31st in a 30-day month), the charge falls on the last day of the month. Annual plans renew on the same calendar date each year.

## Payment methods

TaskLoop accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. ACH transfer and invoicing by purchase order are available on the Team plan for annual contracts of $5,000 or more. TaskLoop does not accept PayPal, cryptocurrency, or wire transfers under $5,000.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, TaskLoop retries on days 1, 3, and 7 after the failure. The workspace owner and billing contacts receive an email after each failed attempt. After the third failure, the workspace is downgraded to read-only: existing data stays intact and viewable, but tasks cannot be created or edited. Read-only workspaces are restored immediately once a successful payment is made. Data is retained for 90 days in read-only state before deletion warnings begin.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated at each charge and available under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Every invoice is downloadable as PDF. Invoices include the workspace name, billing address, seat count, and tax where applicable. Past invoices remain available for 7 years.

## Editing invoice details

Billing address, company name, and VAT/tax ID are editable from Settings > Billing. Changes apply to future invoices only. TaskLoop can reissue the most recent invoice with corrected details on request; older invoices cannot be modified.

## Seats and proration

Adding a seat mid-period charges a prorated amount immediately. Removing a seat issues prorated account credit toward the next invoice (see the refund policy: seat reductions are credit, never cash).

## Tax

Sales tax and VAT are calculated at checkout based on billing address. Customers with a valid VAT ID in the EU can enter it to apply reverse charge.
