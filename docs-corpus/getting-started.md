# Getting Started with TaskLoop

TaskLoop is a project-management SaaS built around boards, tasks, and integrations. This guide covers your first 15 minutes.

## Creating an account

Sign up at app.taskloop.com with a work email address. Verification emails arrive within 2 minutes; if nothing appears, check spam and then use "Resend verification" (limit: 5 resends per hour). Personal email domains (gmail.com, outlook.com) are accepted on the Free plan but blocked on Team plan workspaces that have SSO enforced.

## Your first workspace

Every account belongs to exactly one workspace by default. A workspace holds boards, members, and billing settings. Workspace names must be 3-50 characters. You can rename a workspace at any time from Settings > General; the workspace URL slug updates immediately and the old slug stops resolving after 24 hours.

## Your first board

From the workspace home, click "New board". Choose a template (Kanban, Sprint, Bug Tracker, or Blank). New boards start with three columns: To Do, In Progress, Done. Free plan accounts are limited to 3 boards total; attempting to create a fourth returns an inline error prompting an upgrade.

## Inviting teammates

Settings > Members > Invite. Invitations expire after 7 days. The Free plan allows 5 users per workspace, including the owner. Pending invitations count against that limit.

## Onboarding checklist

New workspaces show a checklist with 5 items: create a board, create a task, invite a teammate, connect an integration, and set a due date. Completing all 5 dismisses the checklist permanently. You can dismiss it early from the checklist's overflow menu.

## Getting help

In-app chat is available on all plans. Response targets differ by plan; see the SLA documentation.
