# Boards and Tasks

## Boards

A board is a collection of columns holding tasks. Each board supports up to 20 columns. Columns can be reordered by drag or via the column menu. Boards can be archived (recoverable for 30 days) or deleted (permanent after a 7-day grace period in Settings > Trash).

Plan limits: Free allows 3 boards per workspace. Pro and Team allow unlimited boards.

## Tasks

A task has a title (required, 1-200 characters), description (Markdown, up to 20,000 characters), assignee, due date, labels, and attachments. Tasks are identified by a per-board key like BUG-42.

- Assignees: up to 10 per task.
- Labels: up to 15 labels per board, 5 per task.
- Attachments: 25 MB per file on Free, 100 MB per file on Pro and Team. Total workspace storage is 2 GB (Free), 50 GB (Pro), 500 GB (Team).
- Subtasks: up to 50 per task, one level deep. TaskLoop does not support nested subtasks beyond one level.

## Comments and mentions

Comments support Markdown and @mentions. Mentioning a user who is not a workspace member triggers an invite prompt rather than a notification. Comments can be edited for 15 minutes after posting; after that, edits show an "edited" marker with a timestamp.

## Search and filters

Board search matches task titles and descriptions. Filters combine assignee, label, due date, and status. Saved filters are per-user, limited to 10 saved filters on Free and 50 on Pro and Team.

## Activity history

Every board keeps an activity log. Retention is 30 days on Free, 1 year on Pro, and 3 years on Team.

## Bulk actions

Select multiple tasks with shift-click to move, label, or assign in bulk. Bulk operations are capped at 100 tasks per action.
